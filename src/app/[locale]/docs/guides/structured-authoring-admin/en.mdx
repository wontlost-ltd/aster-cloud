# Structured Authoring for administrators

**Structured Authoring** adds writing aids to the policy editor: the [Model](/docs/guides/domain-modelling#the-model-tab), [Vocabulary](/docs/guides/domain-vocabulary#the-vocabulary-tab) and [Layout](/docs/guides/display-layouts#create-a-layout-in-the-editor) tabs, the canonical preview, and the matching command palette entries. It also decides who may save a [display layout](/docs/guides/display-layouts).

It does not gate the language. Structs, type aliases, import aliases and vocabulary terms can be written, saved, compiled and run by anyone, with or without Structured Authoring.

## Who can use it

A person can use Structured Authoring in a policy only when all of these hold, checked in this order:

1. **The global switch is on.** It is off by default.
2. **The plan allows it.** Every plan except Free includes it (Trial, Pro, Team, Enterprise). The plan that counts is the policy's: for a personal policy the author's own plan, for a team policy the team owner's plan.
3. **The person has a grant**, from at least one of these sources:
   - a **direct grant** from a platform administrator;
   - a **team grant** to a team the person belongs to, with a role other than viewer;
   - a **delegation** from the owner of a team the person belongs to, with a role other than viewer, while delegation is on for that team and platform-wide.

A grant and the plan are both required: a grant does not replace the plan, and the plan does not replace a grant. A team grant or delegation gives the person the capability everywhere, but the plan is still checked per policy. So a member on the Free plan can use it in the Pro team's policies but not in their own personal policies.

When the check fails, the editor looks exactly as it does without Structured Authoring: the tabs and the preview are not there.

## Turn it on

1. Open the [admin dashboard](/admin). Under **Platform controls**, in **Feature flags**, find **Structured Authoring** and click **Enable**.
2. **Structured Authoring: team owner delegation** is on by default. Turn it off to stop all team owners from delegating; existing delegations then stop applying until you turn it on again.

Switch changes can take up to a minute to reach every server. Turning a switch off and on again does not touch any grant: all grants apply again as before. Each change is recorded in the audit log as `platform_setting.update`.

## Grant to users and teams

Open **Admin → Structured Authoring** (`/admin/capabilities/structured-authoring`).

**Users tab.** Search by email or name. The **Sources** column lists every source through which a person has the capability: `direct`, `team:<team>` or `delegated:<team>`. **Grant** adds a direct grant; **Revoke** removes it. This page only revokes direct grants: a team grant is revoked on the Teams tab, and a delegation by its team owner.

**Teams tab.** For each team:

- **Team grant** gives the capability to every member except viewers.
- **Seat cap** (0 to 10,000) is how many members the team owner may delegate to. **Owner delegation** turns delegation on or off for this team. Click **Save** to apply both.
- **Seats used** counts the active delegations of members who are still in the team, including members who were later made viewers.

A team without a saved seat cap cannot delegate. A seat cap lower than the seats already used is refused; revoke delegations first. Seats are counted and assigned under a lock on the team, so delegations made at the same moment can never take more seats than the cap.

## Team owners delegate seats

The team owner sees **Structured Authoring seats** in the team's settings (only the owner sees it, not team admins). There the owner:

- sees the seats used and the seat cap;
- chooses a member and clicks **Delegate**. Viewers cannot be chosen;
- revokes a delegation to free the seat.

When delegation is paused for the team or switched off platform-wide, the owner cannot add delegations but can still revoke them. A member who is made a viewer keeps the seat but loses access until promoted again. The owner does not see direct grants that a platform administrator gave to members.

## What happens when access ends

| Event | Effect |
|---|---|
| A direct or team grant is revoked | That source stops counting at the person's next request. |
| A delegation is revoked | Same, and the seat is free again. |
| A member leaves or is removed from the team | The team's grant and its delegation stop applying to that person at once. The delegation is revoked and its seat freed. |
| A member is made a viewer | Team grant and delegation are paused; the seat stays taken. Promoting the member back restores them. |
| The team is deleted | Its team grant and all its delegations are revoked, and its seat cap removed. |
| Ownership is transferred | Delegations stay; the new owner manages them. |
| Delegation is turned off (team or platform) | Delegations are paused, not revoked. Turning delegation on restores them. |
| The global switch is turned off, or the plan is downgraded | Nobody can use the capability in the policies concerned. Grants are kept and apply again when the switch or plan comes back. |

In every case, existing content keeps working:

- Policies that use structs, aliases or vocabulary terms stay valid. They compile and run, and they can still be edited and saved, only without the writing aids.
- Saved display layouts can still be viewed by anyone who can read the policy. Execution never reads them.
- When someone without Structured Authoring saves a new version of a policy that has a layout, the save succeeds and the new version has no layout. Earlier versions keep theirs.

An editor that is already open keeps its tabs until the page is reloaded, but the server checks the capability again whenever a save includes or keeps a layout.

## Audit trail

| Action | When |
|---|---|
| `capability.grant` | A direct grant, team grant or delegation is created |
| `capability.revoke` | A grant is revoked; `reason` is `manual`, `member_removed` or `team_deleted` |
| `capability.allotment.update` | A team's seat cap or owner delegation changes, or the team is deleted |
| `platform_setting.update` | A Structured Authoring switch changes |
