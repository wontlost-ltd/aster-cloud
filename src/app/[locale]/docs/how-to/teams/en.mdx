# Invite a team and assign business roles

A team is a shared workspace. Team policies, team API keys and approvals belong to the team rather than to one person, so reviewers and approvers can work on the same rules.

## Create a team

1. Open [Teams](/teams) and click "New Team".
2. Enter a name and a team URL (lowercase letters, digits and single hyphens), then click "Create team".

You become the team owner. Creating a team needs a plan that includes team features. The owner's plan sets the member limit for the team. If you already belong to a team, you can still see it and accept invitations on any plan.

## Invite members

1. Open the team and go to **Members**.
2. Under "Invite New Member", enter the person's email address and choose a role, then click "Send Invitation".
3. The invitation is valid for 7 days. Until it is accepted it is listed under "Pending Invitations", where you can revoke it.

The invited person signs in with the account for that email address. The invitation appears on their dashboard and on the Teams page, where they accept it.

<Callout type="tip" title="Accepting needs the same email">
An invitation can only be accepted by the account whose email it was sent to. If someone signs in with a different address, they are asked to switch accounts.
</Callout>

## Team roles

Each member has one team role. It controls what they can do in the team:

| Role | Can do |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including deleting the team and transferring ownership. There is one owner. |
| Admin | Manage members and invitations, edit team settings, create, edit, delete and approve team policies. |
| Member | View the team, create, edit and run team policies. |
| Viewer | View the team and its policies. |

Admins cannot invite or promote anyone to a role equal to or higher than their own, and cannot change other admins.

## Business roles

A business role is a label such as `Credit Officer` or `Data Protection Officer`. It is separate from the team role. A policy rule can ask for a decision from a specific business role, for example `Verdict.require_approval("Credit Officer", "large_exposure")`. Only a member who holds exactly that business role can approve or reject the request. See [Approvals](/docs/how-to/approvals).

To assign business roles:

1. Open the team's **Members** page.
2. Next to "Business roles" for a member, click "Edit".
3. Enter the roles, separated by commas, and click "Save".

Each role is 1 to 64 printable ASCII characters without commas, and a member can hold up to 16. The match is exact, so use the same spelling as in the policy.

Who can change business roles:

- the owner can change anyone's, including their own;
- an admin can change those of members and viewers, but not their own, the owner's, or another admin's.

<Callout type="warning" title="Business roles grant approval rights">
Giving someone a business role lets them decide approvals that require it. Assign approver roles deliberately, and remove them when a person changes job.
</Callout>
